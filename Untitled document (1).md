# **PART 1 — PRODUCT IDENTITY**

## **1.1 Product Positioning**

### **V1 Positioning**

**AJOCREDIT is a Credit-Building Platform powered by community savings.**

It enables people who already participate in Ajo, Esusu, Adashe and similar savings groups to turn their real savings behaviour into a structured, verifiable financial identity.

### **Long-Term Company Positioning**

**Alternative Credit Infrastructure**

The long-term objective is to build infrastructure that allows financial institutions and other authorized partners to understand financial behaviour that traditional credit systems may not adequately capture.

---

## **1.2 Core Product Promise**

### **External Promise**

> **Turn Your Ajo Into Bank Credit**

### **Internal Product Mission**

> **Build Financial Identity Through Community Savings**

The product therefore moves beyond simply digitising Ajo.

### **Strategic progression**

**Community Savings → Transaction History → Financial Behaviour → Financial Identity → Trust Infrastructure → Credit Infrastructure → Financial Access**

---

## **1.3 Primary V1 Customer**

**Existing Ajo Groups**

The initial target includes:

* Market women  
* Traders  
* Salary earners  
* Cooperative groups  
* Associations  
* Informal savings groups  
* Existing Esusu/Adashe/Ajo participants

### **Example**

**Aunty Bola**

* Group administrator  
* 10 members  
* Each member contributes ₦10,000–₦50,000  
* Weekly or monthly contributions  
* Currently manages the group through WhatsApp, bank transfers and spreadsheets

AJOCREDIT replaces this fragmented process with a structured digital financial system.

---

## **1.4 Core Product Asset**

The fundamental strategic asset is:

### **AJOCREDIT Financial Identity™**

It combines:

1. Identity  
2. Savings behaviour  
3. Contribution reliability  
4. Group participation  
5. Reputation  
6. Verification

This produces an **AJOCREDIT Score** and broader financial behaviour profile.

---

## **1.5 Financial Identity**

A user's Financial Identity will contain:

### **Identity**

* Name  
* Phone number  
* Verification status

### **Savings**

* Total amount saved  
* Average contribution  
* Savings duration  
* Contribution frequency

### **Reliability**

* On-time contribution rate  
* Late contributions  
* Missed contributions  
* Defaults

### **Group Participation**

* Number of groups  
* Active groups  
* Completed groups  
* Participation duration

### **Reputation**

* AJOCREDIT Score  
* Trust Tier  
* Score trend  
* Individual Trust Index

### **Verification**

* AJOCREDIT ID  
* QR verification  
* Shareable profile  
* Financial report  
* Consent-controlled access

---

## **1.6 Revenue Priority**

The long-term revenue architecture is prioritised as follows:

1. **Credit API**  
2. **Institution Portal**  
3. **Loan Marketplace**  
4. **Cooperative SaaS**  
5. **Transaction Fees**  
6. **Reports**

The consumer application therefore becomes the acquisition and data-generation layer, while institutional infrastructure becomes an important long-term monetisation layer.

# **PART 2 — AJO ENGINE & GROUP ARCHITECTURE**

## **2.1 Savings Models**

### **V1**

AJOCREDIT will support:

* **Rotational Ajo**  
* **Target Savings**

### **V2**

Potential expansion into:

* Cooperative lending  
* More sophisticated savings products  
* Institutional cooperative products

---

## **2.2 Group Creation**

An administrator should be able to create a group quickly.

Required information includes:

* Group name  
* Contribution amount  
* Number of members  
* Contribution frequency  
* Start date  
* Payout structure  
* Group rules

The product should make group creation simple enough to complete in approximately one minute.

---

## **2.3 Payout Order**

AJOCREDIT will support three payout-order mechanisms:

### **A. Administrator Assigned**

The administrator establishes the order.

### **B. Random**

The system generates the order.

### **C. Member Voting**

Members vote on the payout sequence according to the group's configured rules.

The selected mechanism becomes part of the group's governance record.

---

## **2.4 Multiple Groups**

A member can belong to **multiple groups simultaneously**.

For example:

> User → Group A \+ Group B \+ Group C

Each group generates additional behavioural history for the user's Financial Identity.

---

## **2.5 Portable Reputation**

A user's financial reputation should not be trapped inside one Ajo group.

Subject to consent and applicable privacy requirements, their verified AJOCREDIT history can follow them across groups.

This creates the foundation for:

**One Person → Multiple Groups → One Financial Identity**

---

## **2.6 Group Trust Score**

In addition to an individual's score, each group will have a:

### **Group Trust Index**

Potential inputs include:

* Contribution consistency  
* Default rate  
* Completion history  
* Member reliability  
* Dispute history  
* Governance compliance  
* Reserve position  
* Group stability

This creates two distinct trust layers:

**Individual Trust Index \+ Group Trust Index**

---

## **2.7 Default Protection**

AJOCREDIT will use a combination of:

* Trust Score  
* Guarantor  
* Security Deposit  
* Group Governance

These mechanisms can be applied according to group rules and risk.

---

## **2.8 Financial Identity Architecture**

The Ajo Engine becomes the data-generating layer for the Financial Identity Engine.

### **Flow**

**Group Participation**

↓

**Contribution Behaviour**

↓

**Verified Transaction History**

↓

**Financial Behaviour**

↓

**AJOCREDIT Financial Identity**

↓

**AJOCREDIT Score \+ Trust Index**

↓

**Financial Access**

# **PART 3 — MONEY ENGINE**

## **3.1 Custody Architecture**

AJOCREDIT should **not automatically be treated as the custodian of customer funds**.

The intended architecture is:

**Member**

↓

**Collection Account**

↓

**Partner Bank Safeguarded Account Structure**

↓

**AJOCREDIT Control / Ledger Layer**

↓

**Payout Engine**

↓

**Beneficiary**

The exact custody, settlement and safeguarding structure must be implemented through appropriately authorised/licensed partners and validated against applicable Nigerian financial regulations.

---

## **3.2 V1 Payment Methods**

V1:

* Card  
* Bank transfer

V2:

* USSD

Additional payment rails can subsequently be introduced.

---

## **3.3 Dedicated Virtual Accounts**

AJOCREDIT will support **dedicated virtual accounts** where appropriate.

This can improve:

* Payment identification  
* Reconciliation  
* Member attribution  
* Group accounting  
* Automated payment matching

---

## **3.4 Contribution and Service Fee**

The member's contribution should remain intact.

Example:

**Contribution:** ₦20,000  
**Service Fee:** ₦200  
**Total Payment:** ₦20,200

The group savings pot receives:

**₦20,000**

The ₦200 is treated separately as the service fee.

The fee must be clearly displayed before payment.

---

## **3.5 Failed Contribution Workflow**

A failed or missing contribution follows a structured escalation:

**Reminder**

↓

**Reminder**

↓

**Grace Period**

↓

**Penalty**

↓

**Default Record**

The exact penalties and timelines are determined by the group's governance rules and applicable legal constraints.

---

## **3.6 Early Exit**

Early exit is **rule-based**.

The applicable treatment depends on:

* Group type  
* Cycle status  
* Whether the member has already received a payout  
* Settlement status  
* Group governance rules

AJOCREDIT should not allow arbitrary withdrawals that could destabilise a live group.

---

## **3.7 Refunds**

Refunds are also rule-based.

Relevant factors include:

* Group type  
* Current cycle  
* Member status  
* Payout status  
* Settlement status  
* Group rules

---

## **3.8 Contribution Protection**

Contribution protection is part of the planned architecture but is **not a mandatory V1 feature**.

Potential future mechanisms include:

* Guarantee arrangements  
* Insurance/protection partners  
* Reserve mechanisms  
* Risk-based contribution protection

---

## **3.9 Core Money Principle**

The product should separate:

### **Money**

from

### **Financial Records**

and

### **Application Logic**

This creates a cleaner architecture:

**Payment Rail → Safeguarded Funds → Ledger → Rules Engine → Payout**

rather than allowing application users or administrators to directly control group funds.

---

# **PART 4 — CREDIT ENGINE**

## **4.1 AJOCREDIT Score**

AJOCREDIT will create a proprietary:

### **AJOCREDIT Score**

Scale:

**0–1000**

It represents a user's observed financial reliability and savings behaviour within the AJOCREDIT ecosystem.

It should not automatically be represented as a legally recognised credit-bureau score unless and until the relevant regulatory/bureau framework supports that claim.

---

## **4.2 Scoring Model**

| Factor | Weight |
| ----- | ----- |
| On-Time Contributions | **35%** |
| Contribution Consistency | **20%** |
| Total Value Saved | **10%** |
| Groups Successfully Completed | **15%** |
| Default History | **10%** |
| Group Reputation | **5%** |
| Identity Verification | **5%** |
| **Total** | **100%** |

---

## **4.3 Score Philosophy**

The score combines three major behavioural dimensions:

### **Financial Reliability**

Does the person pay when required?

### **Savings Behaviour**

How consistently and sustainably does the person save?

### **Community Trust**

How does the person perform within real financial communities?

Therefore:

> **AJOCREDIT Score \= Financial Reliability \+ Savings Behaviour \+ Community Trust**

---

## **4.4 Score Decay**

The score will use:

### **Activity-Based Decay**

Recent behaviour should progressively become more relevant than very old behaviour.

This prevents an individual from permanently benefiting from historical performance while currently demonstrating poor behaviour.

---

## **4.5 Trust Tiers**

| Tier | Score |
| ----- | ----- |
| Bronze | 0–399 |
| Silver | 400–599 |
| Gold | 600–799 |
| Platinum | 800–1000 |

These tiers are descriptive product classifications rather than guarantees of credit approval.

---

## **4.6 Lender Visibility**

An authorised institution should be able to view the user's:

### **Full Financial Identity**

—not merely the score.

Depending on consent and permissions, this can include:

* Contribution history  
* Savings history  
* Reliability  
* Group completion  
* Defaults  
* Trust Index  
* Score trend  
* Verification  
* Relevant reports

This reduces the risk of treating one numerical score as the entire financial story.

---

## **4.7 Consent**

Financial Identity information will only be shared with third parties:

### **With User Consent**

Consent should be:

* Explicit  
* Traceable  
* Purpose-specific  
* Revocable where applicable  
* Logged

---

## **4.8 Credit Passport**

AJOCREDIT will introduce a:

# **Credit Passport**

It will contain:

* Unique AJOCREDIT ID  
* QR verification  
* Shareable profile  
* Financial behaviour report  
* Verification link  
* Consent controls

The Credit Passport becomes a portable representation of verified financial behaviour.

---

## **4.9 Institution Portal**

AJOCREDIT will provide an institutional portal for:

* Banks  
* Microfinance institutions  
* Fintech lenders  
* Cooperatives  
* SACCOs  
* Associations

Institutional users can potentially:

* Verify users  
* Request reports  
* Manage consent  
* Access authorised financial information  
* Use APIs  
* Validate Credit Passports

---

## **4.10 Credit Marketplace**

A credit marketplace is approved as:

### **V3**

It can eventually connect verified users with authorised:

* Banks  
* MFIs  
* Fintech lenders  
* Cooperative lenders  
* Other eligible credit providers

AJOCREDIT itself should not assume lending activities that require separate regulatory authorisation.

# **PART 5 — TRUST ENGINE & FRAUD PROTECTION**

## **5.1 Identity Verification Roadmap**

### **V1**

**Phone OTP**

### **V2**

**Phone \+ BVN**, subject to applicable regulatory and partner framework.

### **V3**

**BVN \+ NIN / stronger identity verification**, where lawful, technically practical and appropriate.

---

## **5.2 Guarantor**

AJOCREDIT will support a:

### **Guarantor Mechanism**

A guarantor can provide an additional trust layer for selected groups, members or risk scenarios.

---

## **5.3 Security Deposit**

A security deposit will be:

### **Optional and Risk-Based**

It should not automatically apply to every member or every group.

Its purpose is to provide an additional protection mechanism where the group's rules and risk structure require it.

---

## **5.4 Group Reserve**

Groups can establish:

### **Optional Group Reserves**

The system should define:

* Who contributes  
* How much is required  
* What qualifies for use  
* Who approves use  
* How reserve transactions are recorded  
* How the reserve is settled

Custody and use must be structured compliantly.

---

## **5.5 Vouching**

AJOCREDIT will support a:

### **Vouching Mechanism**

Members can provide trusted social/financial references within defined rules.

Vouching should not become an uncontrolled popularity system; it must have safeguards against collusion and manipulation.

---

## **5.6 Separate Fraud Score**

AJOCREDIT will maintain:

### **Fraud/Risk Indicator**

separately from:

### **AJOCREDIT Score**

This distinction is important.

A person can have good savings behaviour while exhibiting suspicious technical or transactional activity.

Therefore:

**Credit Behaviour ≠ Fraud Risk**

---

## **5.7 Device Intelligence**

Device intelligence will be incorporated to detect patterns such as:

* Multiple accounts  
* Suspicious device reuse  
* Abnormal login patterns  
* Transaction anomalies  
* Account takeover indicators  
* Coordinated fraudulent activity

The implementation must comply with privacy and data-protection requirements.

---

## **5.8 Group Governance**

Every group can have configurable governance rules including:

* Late fees  
* Grace period  
* Voting threshold  
* Exit policy  
* Payout rules  
* Reserve requirements  
* Default rules  
* Member approval rules

The system should enforce configured rules rather than relying on informal WhatsApp agreements.

---

## **5.9 Dispute Resolution**

AJOCREDIT will provide a formal dispute centre.

### **Workflow**

**Submit Dispute**

↓

**Upload Evidence**

↓

**Review**

↓

**Resolution**

↓

**Audit Trail**

Relevant evidence and decisions should be recorded for future reference.

---

## **5.10 Trust Architecture**

AJOCREDIT will maintain two principal trust measurements:

### **Individual Trust Index**

Measures an individual's behaviour.

### **Group Trust Index**

Measures the reliability and governance quality of a group.

These metrics operate alongside the AJOCREDIT Score and Fraud/Risk Indicator.

# **PART 6 — BUSINESS MODEL & UNIT ECONOMICS**

## **6.1 Core Revenue Model**

The selected model is:

### **Basic Groups Free \+ Premium Paid**

This allows AJOCREDIT to reduce adoption barriers while monetising groups requiring more advanced capabilities.

---

## **6.2 Transaction Fees**

### **Member-Paid Transaction Fee**

The member pays the transaction/service fee.

The fee is:

* Transparent  
* Displayed before payment  
* Separate from the contribution amount

Indicative pricing previously established for modelling:

| Contribution | Indicative Fee |
| ----- | ----- |
| Below ₦10,000 | 1.5% |
| ₦10,000–₦100,000 | 1.0% |
| Above ₦100,000 | 0.75% |

These figures remain **commercial assumptions for testing**, not final prices.

---

## **6.3 Financial Identity Monetisation**

### **Basic Financial Identity**

**Free**

### **Premium**

Paid services can include:

* Enhanced reports  
* Verified reports  
* Advanced verification  
* Institutional-grade documentation

This follows the principle:

> Basic access builds adoption; verified financial intelligence creates monetisation.

---

## **6.4 Credit API**

The Credit API will use:

### **Tiered Pricing**

Illustrative structure:

**Starter**

* Up to 1,000 API requests

**Growth**

* Up to 10,000 requests

**Enterprise**

* Custom volume and institutional requirements

This is strategically important because the API can eventually become a major infrastructure revenue stream.

---

## **6.5 Credit Reports**

### **Basic Report**

**Free**

### **Verified / Institutional Report**

**Paid**

Indicative pricing previously discussed:

* Standard: ₦500  
* Verified: ₦1,500  
* Institutional: ₦5,000

These remain initial commercial assumptions subject to market validation.

---

## **6.6 Cooperative / Association SaaS**

Pricing will be:

### **Tiered According to Membership and Scale**

The system can support different levels based on:

* Number of members  
* Number of groups  
* Transaction volume  
* Administrative requirements  
* Reporting requirements  
* Institutional integrations

An earlier indicative starting point was approximately:

**₦50,000+/month**

for larger cooperative/association deployments, subject to actual pricing research.

---

## **6.7 Data Insights**

AJOCREDIT will **not sell individual financial records**.

The selected model is:

### **Only Anonymised/Aggregated Insights**

and only under:

* Strict governance  
* Applicable law  
* Appropriate consent where required  
* Strong privacy controls

Individual financial data remains protected.

---

## **6.8 High-Volume Groups**

AJOCREDIT will support:

### **Volume-Based Pricing**

Large groups can receive commercial pricing based on transaction volume, membership and service requirements.

This allows the platform to serve:

* Large associations  
* Cooperatives  
* Churches/organisations  
* Professional associations  
* Large informal savings networks  
* Institutional communities

---

## **6.9 Geographic Economics**

The product architecture will be:

### **Global Architecture \+ Country-Specific Economics**

This means the underlying technology can be standardised while adapting:

* Currency  
* Payment rails  
* Regulations  
* Identity systems  
* Credit infrastructure  
* Pricing  
* Tax  
* Local financial practices

for each country.

---

## **6.10 Long-Term Company Model**

The selected strategic model is:

# **Financial Infrastructure Company**

with the:

# **Consumer App as the Front End**

This is a critical distinction.

AJOCREDIT is therefore not intended to remain merely an Ajo-management application.

The consumer app generates verified financial behaviour.

That behaviour creates financial identity.

Financial identity creates trust.

Trust creates institutional financial access.

# **FINAL STRATEGIC DEFINITION**

## **AJOCREDIT is not simply:**

**“An app for managing Ajo.”**

It is designed to become:

> **A financial infrastructure platform that converts real community savings behaviour into verifiable financial identity, trust and eventually access to formal financial services.**

The Ajo is the **entry point**.

The transaction history is the **data layer**.

Financial behaviour is the **evidence**.

Financial Identity is the **core asset**.

Trust is the **bridge**.

Credit is the **access layer**.

And the long-term business is the **infrastructure connecting people, communities and financial institutions**.

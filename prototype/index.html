How leaving early works

Your spot and remaining payments move to someone new your admin invites. No cash back — your past payments already helped others get paid.

You have 7 days to find them — otherwise the group pauses that spot and adjusts. Everyone is told what changed.

Policy details

Replacement inherits slot + schedule; short-handling + resequence if none found; full audit trail.

⚠ Leaving after payout = default

You already collected, so stopping now breaks the group. Score −300, account paused. Our licensed partner may follow up on the balance.

Policy details

Immediate defaulted status, no grace ladder; dispute opened for review; possible partner collections referral.

Removed by admin?

Same rules apply, and you always get a reason plus a way to appeal. Nobody is removed silently.

Dissolve group?

Over 50% cycles done: admin may dissolve with notice (unpaid members refunded pro-rata). Under 50%: majority must agree. Fraud: platform freezes, per-member review.
